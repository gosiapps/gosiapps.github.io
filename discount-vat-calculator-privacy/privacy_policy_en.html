Privacy Policy — Discount & VAT Calculator

Last updated: 2026-10-28

Discount & VAT Calculator ("the App") is a utility app that calculates prices after a discount, tax/VAT, and the original price, from values entered by the user. The App does not require an account and does not directly collect personal information (name, email address, phone number).

Nature of the app

The App calculates the price after a discount (a single one or two applied in sequence), the tax/VAT amount added on top of the discounted price, and, in reverse mode, the original price from a final price and a discount percentage. All calculations run locally on the device, from values entered manually by the user.

Data processed locally on your device

The App stores locally on the device (in the app's internal storage) only:

  • the selected theme (light/dark/system) and the selected interface language,
  • your own custom tax/VAT rate presets, if you set them in Settings (instead of the rates suggested for your region),
  • a history of your calculations (date, entered values, result), visible in the App's "History" screen.

This data is never sent to any server, is never shared with third parties, and never leaves your device. You can delete your calculation history at any time using the "Clear history" button in the App, or delete all of the App's data by uninstalling it.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display a banner ad at the bottom of the calculator screen. The App does not show full-screen (interstitial) ads. For this purpose, the App requests permission to use the advertising identifier (Advertising ID) and connects to the internet.

Data processed by third parties

Google AdMob and related Google services may process the following technical data in order to display and measure the effectiveness of ads:

  • the device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID),
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device data (model, operating system, app version),
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks).

This data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and Google's EU User Consent Policy. The App's publisher does not have access to this data in a form that would allow direct identification of the user.

Your rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • access your data,
  • rectify or erase your data,
  • restrict or object to processing,
  • data portability,
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Because the App does not directly collect personal data, and the prices you enter and your calculation history stay only on your device, the rights above regarding data processed by third parties (AdMob) are exercised through your Google account's and device's ad settings and the tools described in Google's privacy policy.

California users (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA. You can exercise this right in your Android device's or Google account's ad settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of the Google Play Families program. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

Permissions used by the App

  • INTERNET — required to display the banner ad,
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to check network connectivity,
  • AD_ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) — access to the advertising identifier for AdMob.

Data security

We use reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect processed data; however, no method of data transmission or storage is 100% secure.
